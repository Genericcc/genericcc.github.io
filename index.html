Tainted Grail: The Fall of Avalon

Open-world first-person RPG

The one people like to call "Polish Skyrim" — a first-person open world set in a dark reimagining of Arthurian legend. I built a new game mode along with the enemies that populate it, and prototyped gameplay systems to find out what was worth keeping before it went into production. A good part of my time went into refactors and patches, keeping a large, long-running codebase healthy while the game kept shipping.

Bermuda Survivor

Pixel-art action survivor

A pixel-art action survivor that mixes bullet heaven combat with open exploration of the Bermuda Triangle. This one I saw through from the first prototype to release, and touched pretty much every part of it. Gameplay: combat, enemy AI, skills, buffs, inventory, vehicles and bosses. Core: the modifier system, pathfinding and optimization. Visuals and audio: VFX, SFX and shaders.

Stormbinders

Turn-based strategy in the spirit of HoMM 3

Heroes of Might and Magic III was the obvious reference here, only the war is against chaotic magic storms. I built the turn-based combat on a hexagonal grid, worked on world creation and the exploration layer around it, and put together the UI for cities, combat, heroes and menus.
