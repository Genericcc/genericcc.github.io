Polar

City Builder on a polar grid

The goal of this project was to gain experience with DOTS and solidify my knowledge of Zenject. The city is set on a configurable polar grid with multiple levels. Node detection under the mouse is math-based, and the grid has an unmanaged representation for DOTS, which it uses for pathfinding. Buildings and people are Entities spawned on demand, 15 per house in the video, and their paths are calculated using a Burst-compiled IJob. The feature I am currently working on is the pathfinding system for citizens.
